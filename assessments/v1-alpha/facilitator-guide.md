# Facilitator Guide — Services Delivery Assessment

How to run a facilitated workshop for the services-delivery domain.

---

## Pre-workshop (1 week before)

- [ ] Confirm participants (8–15 people: VP Eng, EMs, product, customer success, sponsor)
- [ ] Send pre-read: link to `instrument.yaml` and 5 dimensions
- [ ] Confirm room (physical or virtual) and tools
- [ ] Send calendar invite
- [ ] Prepare score sheets (5 dimensions)

---

## Agenda (3 hours)

| Time | Activity | Lead |
|------|----------|------|
| 0:00 | Welcome, agenda | Facilitator |
| 0:10 | Dimension 1 — Predictability (40 min) | VP Engineering |
| 0:50 | Dimension 2 — Flow (40 min) | Engineering Manager |
| 1:30 | Comfort break | |
| 1:45 | Dimension 3 — Quality (40 min) | QA Lead / EM |
| 2:25 | Dimension 4 — Outcomes (40 min) | Product Lead |
| 3:05 | Dimension 5 — Team Health (25 min) | People Manager |
| 3:30 | Cross-cut + actions (20 min) | All |
| 3:50 | Wrap-up | Facilitator |

---

## Per-dimension script

Same 5-step process:

1. **Context (3 min)** — explain why this dimension matters
2. **Read questions (5–10 min)** — walk through, allow clarifying questions
3. **Individual scoring (5–10 min)** — private scoring
4. **Surface disagreements (10–15 min)** — facilitator drives convergence
5. **Lock scores (5 min)** — sum, compute percentage, mark band

---

## Cross-cut discussion (20 min)

- Are forecasts accurate but quality low?
- Is flow fast but outcomes flat?
- Are outcomes measured but disconnected from delivery?
- Are teams happy but delivery slipping?

---

## Post-workshop

Within 5 working days: fill in gap-analysis-template.md and target-state-template.md, publish to sponsor.

---

## Common challenges in services delivery assessments

- **Forecast denial.** "We don't forecast, we deliver." Counter: discuss commitment reality.
- **Quality metrics bias.** Defect count, not escaped defects. Counter: show escaped defects trend.
- **Outcome attribution confusion.** "We can't attribute revenue to feature X." Counter: experiments help.
- **Team health surveys treated as HR exercise.** Counter: tie to delivery outcomes.
